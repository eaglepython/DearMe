Questions

 

Do I need a cookie banner?

Dear Me doesn't use cookies or store personal data, and visitors can't be followed across days. For most sites that means our analytics alone doesn't need consent. Check with your own legal advisor for your situation.

What counts as a pageview?

Each time a page loads, or the URL changes in a single-page app. Goals and other events don't count toward your limit.

What happens if I go over my limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep counting and remind you to move up a plan. If you stay far over it for a while, we pause new data until you upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in one click. You can export your data first.

Can I use it for client websites?

Yes. Add as many sites as your plan allows, invite clients as viewers, or send them a public read-only link.
