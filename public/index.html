2. Track goals (optional)

          
Count signups, purchases, downloads. They show up under Goals, and you can use them in funnels.

          
<script>window.dearme = window.dearme || function () { (dearme.q = dearme.q || []).push(arguments) }</script>

<button onclick="dearme('Signup', { plan: 'pro' })">Sign up</button>

          
Add data-outbound to the script tag to count clicks on links to other sites.

        

        

          
3. Connect your sales and ads

          
With sales and ad costs, your analyst can tell you which sources, pages and ads actually make money, and warn you when they stop.

          

          
Sales

          
Easiest: send the amount with your purchase goal. Add the order number and it is matched with your shop's records, so nothing is counted twice.

          
dearme('Purchase', { revenue: 49.99, order_id: 'A1001' })

          
Or connect your shop by pasting this address as a webhook:
            Stripe (Developers → Webhooks, events checkout.session.completed, invoice.paid, charge.refunded),
            Shopify (Settings → Notifications → Webhooks, Order payment, JSON) or
            WooCommerce (Settings → Advanced → Webhooks, Order updated).
            Anything else can POST JSON like {"amount": 49.99, "id": "A1001", "source": "google"}.
